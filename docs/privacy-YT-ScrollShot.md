# YT ScrollShot Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-24 | Version: 1.7.1 | Contact: see Store “Support” / https://github.com/YouYuHub/Ytools/issues

YT ScrollShot is a local-only screenshot tool.

1. Data collected by the developer: **None.** No account, no analytics, no tracking, no remote servers. The extension accesses and processes selected website content and current-page metadata only on your device to provide screenshots.
2. Data processed locally (never uploaded):
   - Current tab pixels via `chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab` after you click the extension;
   - Scroll positions/styles to restore the page afterwards;
   - Your options (wait time, quality, range, hide rules, display language) in `chrome.storage.local`;
   - Current job metadata (title, URL, progress) in `chrome.storage.session`.
3. Permissions and why:
   - `activeTab`: read & capture the page you clicked on;
   - `scripting`: inject scroll-area detection and scrolling;
   - `storage`: save the items in (2).
   No `host_permissions`. Protected pages (Edge internals, Web Store, PDF viewer) cannot be captured.
4. Sharing: we do not sell, share or upload pages, images or history to anyone, including the developer.
5. Retention: images live in the result page memory; closing/refreshing the result page discards undownloaded images. Uninstalling removes local options.
6. Children: no personal data is collected from anyone.
7. Changes: material changes will be noted in the Store listing and README.
8. Optional author support: payment images are bundled with the extension and displayed locally. Support is entirely voluntary and does not unlock any features. The extension does not process payments or collect financial information. If you choose to visit an external link or use a payment provider to support the author, that service handles your visit or payment under its own policies; no captured page data is attached to those links.
9. Store privacy disclosures for website content and web history refer to the on-device processing of the page you choose to capture and that page's title and URL. The extension does not read your full browsing history or monitor other tabs.

YT ScrollShot 为纯本地截图工具。

1. 开发者收集的数据：**无**。无账号、无统计、无追踪、无远程服务器。扩展仅为截图功能在你的设备上访问和处理所选网页内容与当前页元数据。
2. 仅在本地处理（不上传）：
   - 点击扩展后，通过 `chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab` 获取当前标签页像素；
   - 记录滚动位置/样式用于事后恢复；
   - 选项（等待时间、清晰度、范围、屏蔽规则、显示语言）存 `chrome.storage.local`；
   - 当前任务元数据（标题、URL、进度）存 `chrome.storage.session`。
3. 权限与用途：
   - `activeTab`：仅在你点击后读取并截图当前网页；
   - `scripting`：注入滚动区域识别与滚动控制；
   - `storage`：保存上述本地项。
   无 `host_permissions`。Edge内置页、扩展商店、PDF阅读器等受保护页面无法截图。
4. 共享：不向任何人出售、共享或上传页面、图片、浏览记录，包括开发者本人。
5. 保留：图片在结果页内存中，关闭/刷新结果页即释放未下载图片。卸载即删除本地选项。
6. 儿童：不收集任何人的个人信息。
7. 变更：重要变更将在商店说明与README中注明。
8. 自愿支持作者：收款码图片随扩展打包并在本地显示。支持完全自愿，不影响任何功能。扩展不处理付款、不收集财务信息。如你主动打开外部链接或通过支付服务支持作者，相关访问或付款由该服务依照其政策处理；这些链接不附带截图页面的数据。
9. 商店隐私披露中的“网站内容”和“Web 历史记录”指在本机处理你主动选择截图的网页，以及该页面的标题和网址。扩展不读取完整浏览历史，也不监控其他标签页。
